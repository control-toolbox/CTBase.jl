## Release proposal — CTBase v0.30.15 (stable)

**Version.** Last release `0.30.14`; last version in General `0.30.14`. Since `0.30.14`: new features, nothing breaking. By `REL1` (0.Y.Z: the breaking component is Y, and nothing breaks, so the patch moves) and `REL2` (the triple must be above every one released: `0.30.14` is the last), the version is **`0.30.15`**.
**Jump in General (`REL3`).** none.
**Resolves from General alone (`REL4`).** ✅.
**Versions applied.** ct-agents `v0.5.0` (this recipe), ct-conventions `v0.15.0`, CTActions `dc60661` (the release tool).

### What changes since `0.30.14`

- New: `Data.ConstrainedPseudoHamiltonian`, `Data.freeze_multiplier`, `Data.ComposedConstrainedHamiltonian` and the getter `Data.multiplier` (CTModels#536, #663), and the generic function `Data.hamiltonian`.
- New (#666): `Data.AbstractConstrainedPseudoHamiltonian` is an extension point, and the trait joins are public (`Traits.join_time_dependence`, `Traits.join_variable_dependence`). The private `Data._join_td` and `Data._join_vd` are removed.
- Documentation: the Data, Differentiation and Traits guides; the alignment of every table column (`SITE19`, #662, #665); the pin of ct-conventions `v0.15.0`.
- Tests: the code-quality meta test loads `Mooncake` first (#668).
- Breaking: none (a `Non-breaking note (0.30.15)` goes in `BREAKING.md`).
- Not in `[Unreleased]` yet, added by the release PR: none. `facts` listed three candidates: #661 (a formatter run, not user-facing), #663 and the removed private helpers (both already in the changelog).
- Open PRs, not in this release: #642 CompatHelper: bump compat for JET in [extras] to 0.13 (draft).

### What the workflow will do

1. Open the tracking issue `[RELEASE] v0.30.15`, and comment on it at every phase.
2. Open the PR `release/v0.30.15` (`Project.toml`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `BREAKING.md`) after the release gate passed; labels `run breakage`, `run ci github-runner`, `run documentation`, `documentation push preview`, `release: autonomous`.
3. Merge with `release.jl merge` once `release.jl ready` says ready (what actually ran on the head commit: gate, breakage report, every job the release needs).
4. Summarize the checks on the issue.
5. Tag `v0.30.15` and create the GitHub release, notes since `0.30.14`.
6. Post the registration comment, once, and follow the General PR to its merge.
7. Close the issue with a final comment.

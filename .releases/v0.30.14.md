**Resolves from General alone (`REL4`).** ✅.

### What changes since `0.30.13`

- New: `CTBase.Plotting.COLORS`, concrete colours for the bounds, the time lines and the phase labels, the same in every backend (#657).
- Fixed, Plots backend: the k-th curve of an overlay has the same colour in every subplot; `color=` and the other aliases of Plots reach the series; the user's `left_margin`, `bottom_margin` and `margin` are honoured (#657).
- Documentation: the pins of ct-conventions and ct-agents, the docs build command, the preview label (#648, #653, #655).
- Breaking: none (a `Non-breaking note (0.30.14)` goes in `BREAKING.md`).
- Not in `[Unreleased]` yet, added by the release PR: none. The five PRs listed by `facts` are CompatHelper bumps of the docs environment (#639, #643), two formatter runs (#645, #652), and the pin of ct-conventions (#655, already in the changelog).
- Open PRs, not in this release: #642 CompatHelper: bump compat for JET in [extras] to 0.13 (draft).

### What the workflow will do

1. Open the tracking issue `[RELEASE] v0.30.14`, and comment on it at every phase.
2. Open the PR `release/v0.30.14` (`Project.toml`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `BREAKING.md`) after the release gate passed; labels `run breakage`, `run ci github-runner`, `run documentation`, `documentation push preview`, `release: autonomous`.
3. Merge with `release.jl merge` once `release.jl ready` says ready (what actually ran on the head commit: gate, breakage report, every job the release needs).
4. Summarize the checks on the issue.
5. Tag `v0.30.14` and create the GitHub release, notes since `0.30.13`.
6. Post the registration comment, once, and follow the General PR to its merge.
7. Close the issue with a final comment.

### What you will do

- Nothing, in `autonomous` mode, unless something fails: the workflow comments `⏸ waiting for you` on the issue and stops.
- The `[merge approved]` comment on the General PR is not needed (no jump).
